# BigBasket Performance Analysis

I checked our dataset for delivered orders to see how much money each category made compared to our target goals.

---

### Categories Ahead of Target
* **Snacks & Beverages:** Target was ₹450,000, but we made ₹520,000. That means we are **ahead by ₹70,000 (15.56%)**. This category sold really well in cities like Bengaluru and Hyderabad.
* **Fruits & Vegetables:** Target was ₹380,000, and we made ₹415,000. So we are **ahead by ₹35,000 (9.21%)**. People order fresh items super often in cities like Pune.

---

### Categories Behind Target
* **Household Essentials:** Target was ₹600,000, but we only made ₹510,000. That means we are **behind by ₹90,000 (-15.00%)**. Even though single items cost more money here (like ₹130 to ₹390 per item), we didn't sell enough overall.
* **Personal Care:** Target was ₹350,000, but we only got ₹295,000. So we are **short by ₹55,000 (-15.71%)**. This category struggled across all cities.

---

## 2 Recommendations for the Category Team

1. **Put More Marketing and Items into Snacks & Vegetables**
   * Since Snacks & Beverages is **₹70,000 over target** and Fruits & Vegetables is **₹35,000 over target**, we know customers love these items. We should run more ads for them and add more product choices so we can make even more sales.

2. **Talk to Suppliers for Household Essentials & Personal Care**
   * Household Essentials missed the goal by **₹90,000** and Personal Care missed by **₹55,000**. The team should review supplier stock issues and try to get lower prices from vendors so we can offer better discounts or bulk deals to customers.
